## Billing Workflow Lifecycle

Every dollar your practice earns follows a predictable path from the moment a patient is seen to the moment the balance is settled. Understanding each stage helps you identify where delays occur and where automation can save time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Encounter">
    A provider completes a clinical encounter and signs the note. The encounter is flagged as ready for charge capture, triggering a task in the billing queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Charge Capture">
    Your billing team (or the provider directly) attaches diagnosis codes (ICD-10) and procedure codes (CPT/HCPCS) to the encounter. The system validates code combinations and flags any mismatches before a claim is created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim Submission">
    The system assembles a clean claim and routes it through your connected clearinghouse. Claims are transmitted in the ANSI X12 837P (professional) or 837I (institutional) format, depending on your facility type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjudication">
    The payer receives and processes the claim, applying the patient's benefits, deductibles, and co-insurance. The payer returns an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) or Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA/835) with payment details or denial reasons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Posting">
    ERA files are imported automatically and matched to open claims. Payments are posted to the correct line items, contractual adjustments are applied, and any patient responsibility balance is calculated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Patient Statement">
    After the insurance balance is resolved, the system generates a patient statement for any remaining balance. Statements can be mailed, emailed, or made available in the online patient portal for payment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Electronic claim submission requires an active clearinghouse connection. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Clearinghouse** to configure your connection before submitting claims electronically. Without this connection, claims must be printed and mailed as paper claims.
</Note>

## Key Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **ERA / 835** | Electronic Remittance Advice — the electronic file a payer sends to explain how a claim was paid or denied. |
| **837P / 837I** | ANSI X12 electronic claim formats for professional (physician) and institutional (hospital/facility) billing. |
| **Clearinghouse** | A third-party service that translates and routes claims between your EHR and payers, performing scrubbing and validation along the way. |
| **Adjudication** | The payer's process of reviewing a claim and determining the amount to pay based on the patient's plan and benefits. |
| **AR (Accounts Receivable)** | Money owed to your practice — broken down by payer AR (insurance) and patient AR (patient balances). |
| **Timely Filing** | Each payer sets a deadline for claim submission after the date of service. Missing it results in an automatic denial. |
