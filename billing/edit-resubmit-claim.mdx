keywords: []
---

Claims in Processing, Completed, or Denied status cannot be edited directly. Return the claim to **Pending**, correct the source appointment, client, provider, insurance, service, or diagnosis information, clear warnings, preview the form, and file again. Confirm whether the payer requires a corrected or replacement claim.
