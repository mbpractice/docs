title: "Manage Diagnoses and Treatment Plans"
sidebarTitle: "Manage Diagnoses and Treatment Plans"
description: "Add diagnoses and manage treatment plans."
---

Add diagnosis codes from the client's Diagnosis tab or a session note and arrange them correctly. The first diagnosis is used as primary on the CMS-1500 claim. Create plans under **Add Note → Diagnosis → Treatment Plan**; when enabled, finalized sections can be locked and sent for client signature.
