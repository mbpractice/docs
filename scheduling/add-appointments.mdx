title: "Add Appointments to the Calendar"
sidebarTitle: "Add Appointments to the Calendar"
description: "Schedule client, recurring, telehealth, and non-client appointments."
keywords: []
---

Select a date and time on the calendar or use **New Session**. For a client appointment, choose the client, room, service, price, date, time, and length. Enable **Telehealth Reminder** or recurrence when needed, then select **Add Appointment**. For blocked time or meetings, use the **Non-Client Appointment** tab.
