title: "Configure Appointment Reminders"
sidebarTitle: "Configure Appointment Reminders"
description: "Enable text, email, and telehealth reminders."
keywords: []
---

Review the client's contact and notification settings to enable text or email reminders. For MBP Telehealth, turn on **Telehealth Reminder** when scheduling; the secure link is sent approximately 20–30 minutes before the appointment. Confirm the client's contact information and consent.
