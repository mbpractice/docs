Waitlists and recall campaigns are two powerful tools for keeping your schedule full, reducing care gaps, and ensuring patients receive timely follow-up. Waitlists capture patients who want to be seen sooner than your current availability allows. Recall campaigns proactively reach out to patients who are due for preventive care, annual exams, or chronic disease management visits. Together, they help your practice maintain high patient engagement and minimize gaps in care.

<Tip>
Enable automated recall messaging to let the system handle outreach without manual effort. When a patient in a recall campaign does not respond within a set window, the system can automatically send a follow-up message via SMS and email — saving your staff hours of phone time each week.
</Tip>

## Waitlists

### Adding a Patient to the Waitlist

When a patient requests an earlier appointment than your next available opening, add them to the waitlist so you can fill cancellations quickly.

To add a patient to the waitlist:

1. Navigate to **Schedule** → **Waitlist** tab, or click **Add to Waitlist** directly from an appointment booking panel when no suitable slot is available.
2. Click **+ Add to Waitlist**.
3. Search for and select the **patient**.
4. Select the **provider** or, if the patient is flexible, toggle **Any Provider** to allow the first available match.
5. Choose the **visit type** the patient needs.
6. Set the patient's **preferred date range** — the earliest date they can come in and, optionally, a latest date (after which the waitlist entry should expire).
7. Set **preferred times of day** if the patient has scheduling constraints: Morning, Afternoon, or Any.
8. Add an optional **note** (e.g., "Patient traveling until the 15th", "Needs ASL interpreter").
9. Click **Save to Waitlist**.

The patient now appears in the waitlist queue, sorted by the date they were added. The waitlist view shows each patient's name, requested visit type, preferred availability window, and how many days they have been waiting.

### Managing the Waitlist

Use the **Waitlist** tab under **Schedule** to view and manage all active waitlist entries. You can filter the list by provider, visit type, or date range to focus on the most relevant entries.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Patient** | Patient name and MRN |
| **Provider** | Requested provider (or "Any") |
| **Visit Type** | Type of appointment requested |
| **Added** | Date the patient was added to the waitlist |
| **Preferred Window** | Earliest and latest acceptable dates |
| **Days Waiting** | Number of calendar days since added |
| **Status** | Active, Offered, Booked, or Expired |

To **remove a patient** from the waitlist manually (e.g., they found an appointment elsewhere), click the patient's row and select **Remove from Waitlist**. Choose a removal reason and click **Confirm**.

### Notifying Waitlisted Patients When a Slot Opens

When an appointment is cancelled or a new slot becomes available, your EHR can automatically identify and notify the best-matched waitlisted patients.

**Automatic notification (recommended):**

When a cancellation occurs, the system scans the waitlist for patients who match the freed slot's provider, visit type, and date. Matching patients receive an automated notification — via SMS and email — with a link to claim the slot through the patient portal. The slot is held for **2 hours** (configurable in Settings) while the patient responds.

- If the patient accepts, the appointment is booked automatically.
- If the patient does not respond within the hold window, the system notifies the next matching patient.
- If no waitlisted patient claims the slot within the hold window, it returns to open availability.

**Manual notification:**

To manually offer a slot to a specific waitlisted patient:

1. Open the cancellation or open slot on the calendar.
2. Click **Fill from Waitlist**.
3. The system displays the top-matched waitlisted patients for this slot. Click **Notify** next to the patient you want to contact.
4. A pre-populated message is sent to the patient. You can edit the message before sending.
5. The waitlist entry status updates to **Offered**. If the patient books, it moves to **Booked**. If they do not respond, you can offer the slot to the next patient.

## Recall Campaigns

Recall campaigns allow you to proactively reach out to patient populations who are due for specific visits — annual physicals, flu shots, diabetic eye exams, mammograms, and more. Instead of relying on patients to remember to schedule, your practice initiates the outreach.

### Setting Up a Recall Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Recall Campaigns">
    Go to **Schedule** → **Recall Campaigns**. You will see a list of any existing campaigns. Click **+ New Campaign** to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and Describe the Campaign">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** (e.g., "Annual Physical – Due Q1 2026") and an optional **internal description** to help staff understand the campaign's purpose. Select the **Campaign Category**:

    - Preventive Care
    - Chronic Disease Management
    - Immunization
    - Screening
    - Follow-Up
    - Other
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the Target Patient Population">
    Use the **Patient Filters** panel to build the criteria that determine which patients are included:

    - **Age range** (e.g., patients aged 50–75)
    - **Biological sex** (e.g., for mammogram recalls)
    - **Diagnosis / problem list** (e.g., patients with active Type 2 Diabetes ICD-10 E11.x)
    - **Last visit date range** (e.g., last visit more than 11 months ago)
    - **Insurance payer** (e.g., Medicare patients only)
    - **Primary provider** (e.g., only patients assigned to Dr. Patel)
    - **Care gap** (e.g., patients flagged for a specific quality measure gap)

    As you add filters, the **Estimated Patient Count** updates in real time so you can gauge the campaign's reach before launching.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Visit Type and Scheduling Window">
    Choose the **Visit Type** patients should book (e.g., Annual Wellness Exam). Set the **scheduling window** — the date range during which you want patients to schedule their appointment. The system uses this to guide patients when they click the booking link in the recall message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Outreach Messaging">
    In the **Messaging** tab, set up the patient-facing communication:

    - **Channel**: SMS, Email, or Both (recommended)
    - **Message template**: Choose from a pre-built template or write a custom message. Use merge fields to personalize: `{{patient_first_name}}`, `{{provider_name}}`, `{{practice_name}}`, `{{booking_link}}`.
    - **Send date**: When the first message should be sent.
    - **Follow-up reminders**: Toggle **Automated Follow-Up** to send a second message to patients who have not responded within a configurable number of days (default: 14 days). You can set up to three follow-up messages.
    - **Stop condition**: The campaign stops messaging a patient as soon as they book an appointment — they will not receive further reminders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Launch">
    Click **Preview Campaign** to review:

    - The number of patients who meet the criteria
    - A sample of the outreach message as the patient will see it
    - The send schedule for initial and follow-up messages

    When everything looks correct, click **Launch Campaign**. The campaign status changes to **Active** and outreach begins on the configured send date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Monitoring Campaign Performance

Track the effectiveness of your recall campaigns from the **Recall Campaigns** dashboard. Click any active or completed campaign to view its performance metrics:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Patients Targeted** | Total patients who met the filter criteria at campaign launch |
| **Messages Sent** | Number of outreach messages delivered |
| **Response Rate** | Percentage of recipients who clicked the booking link |
| **Appointments Booked** | Number of appointments successfully scheduled through the campaign |
| **Conversion Rate** | Percentage of targeted patients who booked an appointment |
| **Opt-Outs** | Patients who unsubscribed from campaign messaging |

Use this data to refine future campaigns — adjusting message timing, content, or patient targeting criteria to improve response rates over time.

### Pausing or Stopping a Campaign

To pause a campaign (e.g., during a practice closure), click the campaign name and select **Pause Campaign**. No further messages are sent while the campaign is paused. Click **Resume** to restart outreach.

To stop a campaign entirely, click **End Campaign**. All scheduled messages are cancelled. Appointments already booked through the campaign are not affected.
