The EHR Platform is organized around a persistent left-side navigation bar that gives you instant access to every major module — regardless of where you are in the application. Once you understand the layout, you'll be able to move between a patient's chart, the appointment schedule, a billing queue, and your clinical inbox in just a few clicks. This guide introduces each navigation area, explains what lives there, and highlights the productivity tools available from any screen.

## Main Navigation Areas

The sidebar divides the platform into six top-level modules. Each module opens its own sub-navigation and content area without requiring a full page reload.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Dashboard" icon="house">
    Your home screen. Displays today's appointment schedule, a summary of unsigned clinical notes, outstanding billing tasks, recent patient activity, and any system alerts requiring your attention. Providers and front-desk staff see different dashboard widgets based on their assigned role.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Patients" icon="users">
    Access the full patient roster, search for individual charts, register new patients, and manage demographics, insurance, allergies, medications, problem lists, and visit history. The patient chart view consolidates every piece of clinical and administrative data for a single patient.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedule" icon="calendar">
    View and manage the practice calendar in day, week, or provider-grid layouts. Create, reschedule, and cancel appointments, manage provider availability blocks, configure appointment types, and check in patients as they arrive. Drag-and-drop rescheduling is supported in the week view.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clinical" icon="stethoscope">
    Access your clinical inbox — including incoming lab results, imaging reports, referral responses, and patient messages — alongside your list of open encounters awaiting documentation. Start new encounter notes, review and sign pending notes, issue orders, and manage e-prescriptions from this module.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Billing" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Manage your practice's revenue cycle end-to-end. Create and scrub claims, track submission status with payers, post ERA/EOB payments, manage patient balances and statements, run financial reports, and configure fee schedules and payer contracts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="gear">
    Configure every aspect of your practice account: general practice information, provider profiles, user accounts and roles, clinical templates, notification preferences, integrations, and compliance documents. Access is restricted to users with the **Admin** or **Practice Manager** role.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Using the Global Search Bar

The global search bar sits at the top of every screen. Press **/** on any page to focus it instantly, or click the magnifying glass icon in the top navigation strip.

You can search across multiple record types simultaneously:

- **Patients** — Search by name, date of birth, MRN, or phone number. Results link directly to the patient's chart.
- **Appointments** — Find upcoming or past appointments by patient name or appointment ID.
- **Clinical Documents** — Locate signed notes, lab results, and uploaded files by keyword or date range.
- **ICD-10 / CPT Codes** — Look up diagnosis and procedure codes while documenting an encounter or creating a claim.
- **Settings & Help** — Type a setting name (e.g., "timezone") or a help topic to jump directly to that page.

Results appear in a grouped dropdown as you type. Press **Enter** to open the full search results page with filtering and sorting options. The platform retains your five most recent searches for quick re-access.

<Info>
  Global search respects your role-based permissions. Users without access to the Billing module, for example, will not see claim or payment results in their search output.
</Info>

## Notifications and Alerts

The bell icon in the top-right corner of the navigation bar displays a badge count when you have unread notifications. Click it to open the notification panel.

Notifications are grouped into three priority levels:

- **Alerts (red)** — Require immediate action, such as a critical lab result flagged by your lab interface, an overdue controlled substance prescription review, or a failed claim submission that needs correction before the payer deadline.
- **Tasks (yellow)** — Items awaiting your attention without a hard deadline, such as an unsigned encounter note, a referral response requiring acknowledgment, or a patient message needing a reply.
- **Updates (blue)** — Informational notices, including completed report exports, successful ERA postings, appointment reminder delivery confirmations, and platform release notes.

Click any notification to navigate directly to the relevant record or task. Mark individual notifications as read by clicking the checkmark, or clear all notifications with the **Mark All Read** button at the top of the panel.

<Warning>
  Critical lab result alerts (red) are also surfaced on your Dashboard and inside the patient's chart. Do not rely solely on the notification panel for time-sensitive clinical alerts — check your Dashboard and Clinical inbox at the start of each session.
</Warning>

You can customize which events trigger notifications for your account under **Settings → Notifications → My Preferences**. Practice administrators can configure organization-wide notification defaults under **Settings → Notifications → Practice Defaults**.
