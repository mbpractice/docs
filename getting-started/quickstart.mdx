This quickstart walks you through the essential steps to get your practice live on the EHR Platform. By the end, you'll have a fully configured practice account, your first patient record on file, an appointment on the calendar, and a clinical note in progress — giving your care team everything they need to begin seeing patients.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.ehrplatform.com/signup](https://app.ehrplatform.com/signup) and enter your name, work email address, and a secure password. You'll receive a verification email — click the confirmation link to activate your account.

    Once verified, you'll be prompted to select your practice type (e.g., primary care, specialty, behavioral health) and your approximate patient volume. This helps the platform pre-configure sensible defaults for your workflows.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Up Your Practice">
    After logging in for the first time, the **Practice Setup Wizard** launches automatically. Provide your practice name, primary address, National Provider Identifier (NPI), Tax ID, and contact details. These details appear on patient-facing documents, superbills, and CMS-1500 claim forms.

    Configure your timezone and date/time preferences in **Settings → Practice → Locale** so that appointments and timestamps reflect your local time correctly. For a full walkthrough of every setting, see the [Account Setup](/getting-started/account-setup) guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add Your First Patient">
    Navigate to **Patients → New Patient** in the left sidebar. Fill in the required fields — legal name, date of birth, sex at birth, and at least one contact method (phone or email). You can also capture insurance information, emergency contacts, and consent documents at this stage, or return to complete them later.

    Once saved, the patient's chart opens automatically. You'll see an empty timeline ready to receive encounters, orders, and documents.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Schedule an Appointment">
    Open the **Schedule** module and click **+ New Appointment**. Select your newly created patient from the search field, choose a provider and appointment type, and pick an available time slot from the calendar view. Click **Confirm** to save the appointment.

    The patient receives an automated confirmation via their preferred contact method if reminders are enabled in your practice settings. You can manage reminder templates under **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start Documenting a Clinical Encounter">
    From the **Schedule** view, locate the appointment you just created and click **Start Encounter**. The clinical documentation workspace opens with your default note template pre-loaded. Fill in the Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan (SOAP) sections, add any diagnoses using ICD-10 search, and attach relevant orders or prescriptions.

    When you're ready, click **Sign Note** to finalize the encounter. Signed notes are locked for editing but can be amended with a co-signer or addendum. The completed note is immediately visible in the patient's chart timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>
