Adding a new team member to your EHR takes only a few minutes. You send an email invitation, the staff member sets their own password, and they land in the system with the exact role and permissions you chose — no IT ticket required. This guide walks you through the full lifecycle of a staff account: inviting, onboarding, and eventually deactivating users when they leave your practice.

<Tip>
  If you're onboarding a large practice or migrating from another system, you can import multiple users at once using the **Bulk Import** feature under **Settings → Users → Import CSV**. Download the template, fill in each staff member's name, email address, and role, then upload the file. Invitations are sent automatically to every valid entry.
</Tip>

## Inviting a New Staff Member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open User Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the left sidebar, then select **Users & Roles → Users**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite User">
    Click the **Invite User** button in the top-right corner of the Users page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Fill in the staff member's **first name**, **last name**, and **email address**. Select their **role** from the dropdown (see [Roles & Permissions](/user-management/roles-and-permissions) for a full breakdown of what each role can access).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set optional preferences">
    Optionally, assign the user to a specific **location** (for multi-site practices) and add them to any relevant **care teams**. You can also add a personal note that will appear in the invitation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The staff member receives an email with a secure, time-limited link to set their password and complete their profile. The invitation link expires after **72 hours**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the staff member accepts the invitation and completes setup, their status changes from **Pending** to **Active** and they can log in immediately.

## Managing Pending Invitations

You can view all outstanding invitations from **Settings → Users → Pending Invitations**. The table shows the invitee's email address, the role they were assigned, who sent the invitation, and when it was sent.

### Resending an Invitation

If a staff member didn't receive their invitation email or the link has expired, you can send a fresh one:

1. Go to **Settings → Users → Pending Invitations**.
2. Find the staff member's entry and click the **⋯** menu on their row.
3. Select **Resend Invitation**. A new 72-hour link is generated and sent immediately. The old link is invalidated.

### Cancelling an Invitation

To cancel an invitation before it is accepted, click **⋯ → Cancel Invitation** on the pending entry. The link is revoked instantly. You can re-invite the same email address at any time.

## Deactivating a User

When a staff member leaves your practice, deactivate their account promptly to remove their access to patient records and all other system data.

<Warning>
  Before deactivating a user, review their open patient assignments, incomplete encounter notes, and any pending prescription or lab order tasks. Deactivating a user does **not** automatically reassign these items. Unaddressed tasks may delay patient care. Reassign open work to another active provider or staff member before proceeding.
</Warning>

To deactivate a user:

1. Go to **Settings → Users** and find the staff member.
2. Click their name to open their profile, then click **Deactivate User** at the bottom of the page.
3. Confirm the action in the dialog. You can optionally add a note (for example, the last day of employment) that is recorded in the audit log.

Deactivated users cannot log in and do not appear in scheduling or assignment dropdowns. Their historical activity — including all notes, orders, and audit entries — is preserved in full and remains accessible to Administrators for compliance and continuity of care.

### Reactivating a User

If you need to restore access (for example, a contractor who returns), go to **Settings → Users**, use the **Status** filter to show **Inactive** users, open their profile, and click **Reactivate User**. Their role and permissions are restored exactly as they were before deactivation.

<Note>
  Your EHR retains a complete, tamper-evident audit log of all user management actions — invitations sent, roles changed, and accounts deactivated — with timestamps and the identity of the administrator who made each change. This log is available under **Settings → Audit Log** and can be exported for compliance reviews.
</Note>
