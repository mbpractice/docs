Protecting patient privacy is a core responsibility of every healthcare provider, and your EHR platform is designed to support that obligation at every step. This page explains how patient data is owned and controlled, how long it is retained, how you can export it, and how to respond when patients exercise their legal rights to access or delete their information. Understanding these policies helps you remain compliant with HIPAA, state privacy laws, and emerging patient rights regulations.

## Patient Data Ownership

Your patients own their health data. As the covered entity, your organization holds that data in trust and is responsible for its stewardship. Your EHR platform acts as a data processor — storing and organizing the data on your behalf — but does not use, sell, or share patient information for any commercial purpose. You retain full control over your organization's data at all times.

Key principles governing data ownership on the platform:

- **No third-party data sales.** Patient data is never sold, rented, or monetized in any form.
- **No use for platform training or analytics.** De-identified or patient-level data is not used to train models or build platform-wide analytics without your explicit opt-in.
- **Data portability.** You can export your complete dataset at any time without restriction or penalty.
- **Data isolation.** Your organization's data is logically isolated from all other tenants on the platform.

## Data Export and Portability

You can export a full copy of any patient record — or your entire organization's dataset — at any time. Exports are provided in standard interoperable formats (FHIR R4, CCD/C-CDA, and CSV) to support continuity of care and transitions to other EHR systems.

### Requesting a Full Patient Record Export

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Patient Chart">
    Navigate to **Patients** in the main menu and search for the patient by name, date of birth, or MRN. Open the patient's chart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the Export Option">
    In the patient chart, click the **Actions** menu (the three-dot icon in the upper right corner) and select **Export Patient Record**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Export Format and Scope">
    Select the format for the export:
    - **FHIR R4 (JSON)** — recommended for transitions to another EHR or for API-based integrations
    - **CCD/C-CDA (XML)** — required for Meaningful Use / USCDI compliance and most referral workflows
    - **CSV** — suitable for spreadsheet review or custom data analysis

    Choose the date range and data categories to include (demographics, encounters, medications, labs, imaging, billing history, documents).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and Generate">
    Click **Generate Export**. For large records, the export runs in the background and you will receive an in-app notification and email when it is ready — typically within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the Export Package">
    Return to **Actions → Export History** or follow the link in your notification email to download the encrypted export package. The download link is valid for **72 hours**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To export your **entire organization's dataset** (for migration or backup purposes), go to **Settings → Data Management → Bulk Export** and follow the same format selection steps. Bulk exports may take several hours for large organizations and are delivered as a secure, encrypted download.

## Right to Access: Patient Data Requests

Under HIPAA, patients have the right to access their own health records. When a patient submits a formal records request, you are generally required to fulfill it within **30 days** (with a possible 30-day extension if you notify the patient in writing).

Your EHR platform helps you manage these requests efficiently:

1. **Log the request** in the patient's chart under **Documents → Patient Rights Requests**. Record the date received, the patient's stated purpose, and the format they prefer.
2. **Generate the export** using the steps above. Select the data categories the patient is entitled to receive.
3. **Deliver the records** via your practice's preferred secure method — direct download link, mailed encrypted USB, or secure email. The platform's **Secure Messaging** feature can deliver records directly to a patient's portal account.
4. **Document completion** by marking the request as fulfilled in the patient rights log, along with the delivery date and method.

If a patient requests an amendment to their record, you may accept or deny the amendment. Either way, document your decision and attach any written statement from the patient in the patient rights log.

## Data Retention Periods

The platform enforces minimum retention schedules, but you are responsible for knowing and applying the retention rules that apply in your state and specialty.

| Data Category | Minimum Retention Period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Adult patient records | 7 years from last encounter | Some states require longer periods |
| Pediatric patient records | Until age 21 + 7 years | Varies significantly by state |
| Billing and claims records | 7 years | Required for Medicare/Medicaid audit purposes |
| Audit logs | 7 years | See [Audit Logs](/compliance/audit-logs) |
| Prescriptions | 10 years (controlled substances may differ) | DEA and state pharmacy board rules apply |
| Imaging studies | 7 years; pediatric until age 21 + 7 years | Consult your radiology or imaging vendor |

<Info>
  Retention periods vary by state, specialty, and payer. For example, some states require adult records to be kept for 10 years, and several states have specific rules for mental health, substance use disorder, and reproductive health records. Consult legal counsel familiar with your state's healthcare regulations.
</Info>

## Account Deletion and Data Purge

If you cancel your subscription or wish to permanently delete all organizational data, the following process applies:

1. **Submit a deletion request** to support via **Settings → Organization → Account Management → Request Account Deletion**, or by emailing support with your organization name and account ID.
2. **30-day recovery window.** All data enters a soft-deleted state and is immediately inaccessible, but can be restored upon request during this window.
3. **Permanent deletion.** After 30 days, all patient records, user data, configuration, and associated files are permanently purged from active systems. Data is removed from encrypted backups on the following backup rotation cycle (typically within 90 days of the initial deletion request).
4. **Deletion certificate.** Upon request, a written certificate of data deletion is provided for your compliance records.

<Warning>
  **Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone** once the 30-day recovery window has elapsed. Before submitting a deletion request, export any records you need to retain for compliance, continuity of care, or legal hold purposes. Failure to retain records for the minimum required period may expose your organization to regulatory and legal liability.
</Warning>

## Subprocessors and Third-Party Data Handling

Your EHR platform relies on a limited set of trusted subprocessors to deliver specific features. Each subprocessor is bound by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) that requires them to handle PHI only as directed, maintain appropriate security safeguards, and not use your data for any independent purpose.

| Subprocessor | Purpose | Data Involved | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reference Lab Networks (e.g., Quest, LabCorp) | Electronic lab order routing and result delivery | Patient demographics, order data, results | United States |
| Clearinghouse Partners (e.g., Availity, Change Healthcare) | Insurance claim submission and eligibility verification | Patient demographics, insurance info, claims data | United States |
| Cloud Infrastructure Provider | Hosting, storage, and compute | All platform data (encrypted) | United States (multi-region) |
| E-Prescribing Network (Surescripts) | Prescription routing to pharmacies | Patient demographics, medication orders | United States |
| Secure Email / Fax Gateway | Delivery of clinical documents and referrals | Clinical document content | United States |

You can request the full, current list of subprocessors at any time by navigating to **Settings → Organization → Compliance → Subprocessor List** or by emailing privacy@ehrplatform.com.

If a new subprocessor is added that processes PHI, you will be notified by email at least **30 days in advance**, giving you the opportunity to raise objections or terminate the service if the new subprocessor is incompatible with your compliance requirements.
