---
title: "Set a Professional's Default Rates"
sidebarTitle: "Set a Professional's Default Rates"
description: "Assign default services and prices to a professional."
keywords: []
---

Go to **Settings → Account Settings → Staff & Users**, select the professional, and open **Default Rates**. Choose one or more services, enter the appropriate rate for each, and save. Appointment-specific prices can still be adjusted.
