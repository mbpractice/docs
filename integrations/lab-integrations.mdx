Lab integrations eliminate the paper requisition workflow entirely. Once you connect a reference laboratory, clinicians can place orders electronically from inside the encounter note, and results flow back into the patient chart automatically — no manual entry, no fax retrieval, no risk of a result getting lost on a desk. Your team receives in-app and optional SMS notifications the moment results are available, with critical values flagged for immediate attention.

## Supported Lab Networks

The following lab networks are available as pre-built, certified connectors:

- **Quest Diagnostics** — direct electronic data interchange (EDI) connection with order acknowledgment and result delivery.
- **LabCorp** — bidirectional HL7 v2 interface supporting orders, results, and specimen tracking updates.
- **Sonic Healthcare** — regional and national network connection with compendium-level order support.
- **Other reference labs** — any lab that supports the **HL7 v2.x** messaging standard can be connected using the generic HL7 interface. This includes hospital outreach labs, specialty labs, and regional networks.

## Connect a Reference Lab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integration Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Lab Integrations**. You will see all currently connected labs and a list of available pre-built connectors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Lab">
    Click **Add Lab** and choose your reference lab from the marketplace. If your lab is not listed, select **Custom HL7 Interface** to set up a generic connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Lab Credentials">
    Enter the account credentials provided by your lab representative. For Quest and LabCorp, this includes your **Client Account Number** and **Site ID**. For the generic HL7 interface, you will need the lab's **receiving IP address**, **port**, and **facility identifiers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import the Order Compendium">
    Click **Sync Compendium** to download the lab's current test catalog. This populates the orderable test list that clinicians see when placing orders. Compendium updates sync automatically on a weekly schedule, or you can trigger a manual sync at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map Providers and Accounts">
    Assign each provider in your practice to the appropriate lab account and NPI for billing purposes. You can configure a practice-level default and override it per provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a Test Order">
    Use the **Send Test Order** button to transmit a test message to the lab's interface and confirm a successful acknowledgment (HL7 ACK) is returned. The connection status will update to **Active** once the handshake is confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Connecting a lab using the **Custom HL7 Interface** requires technical configuration at both the EHR and the lab's interface engine. Work with your lab's EDI or interface team to exchange facility identifiers, agree on message versions (typically HL7 v2.3 or v2.5.1), and configure the network pathway (VPN tunnel or direct TCP connection). Contact EHR support to engage the interface implementation team if you need assistance.
</Note>

## How Results Flow into the Patient Chart

Once a lab integration is active, the result lifecycle is fully automated:

1. A clinician places an order from the **Orders** section of an encounter note or from the patient chart's **Lab Orders** tab.
2. The order is transmitted electronically to the lab. An **order acknowledgment** appears in the chart within seconds, confirming receipt.
3. The lab processes the specimen and transmits the result back as an HL7 ORU message.
4. The result is parsed, structured, and attached to the originating order in the patient's chart under **Results → Lab Results**.
5. Numeric values outside the lab's reference ranges are automatically flagged as **Low**, **High**, or **Critical** based on the lab's reference data.
6. The ordering provider and any configured routing recipients receive an in-app notification and, if enabled, an SMS or email alert.

Results are stored as structured data, which means they are searchable, trendable, and available for use in clinical decision support rules.

## Configuring Result Routing

By default, lab results are routed to the ordering provider. You can customize routing in **Settings → Integrations → Lab Integrations → Result Routing**:

- **Order-based routing** — results always go to the provider who placed the order. This is the default.
- **Panel-based routing** — route specific test types (e.g., all pathology results) to a designated provider or care team regardless of who ordered them.
- **Location-based routing** — route results to the provider on duty at a specific location at the time of result receipt.
- **Shared inbox** — add a clinical staff inbox so that MAs or nurses can perform a first review before results are routed to the provider.

To add or edit a routing rule, click **Add Routing Rule** in the Result Routing settings, choose the scope (practice, location, or provider), and select the recipients.

<Tip>
  Enable **Critical Result SMS Alerts** for all ordering providers so they receive an immediate text message when a result is flagged as critical — regardless of whether they are currently logged in. To set this up, go to **Settings → Notifications → Lab Results → Critical Values**, toggle **SMS Alert** on, and confirm each provider's mobile number. Providers can also configure this themselves from their own notification preferences under **My Account → Notifications**.
</Tip>
