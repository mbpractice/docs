Your EHR is designed to work within a broader healthcare ecosystem — not in isolation. By connecting to reference labs, health information exchanges (HIEs), billing clearinghouses, telehealth platforms, and your own internal systems via REST API, you eliminate duplicate data entry, reduce errors, and give your clinical team a complete view of every patient at the point of care. All integrations are managed centrally from **Settings → Integrations**, so your administrators maintain full visibility and control over every connection.

## Managing Integrations

All integrations are authorized and managed from **Settings → Integrations** in your administrator account. From this dashboard you can:

- **Browse** available pre-built connectors in the Integration Marketplace.
- **Authorize** new connections using OAuth, API keys, or HL7 interface credentials depending on the integration type.
- **Monitor** connection health, sync status, and error logs in real time.
- **Revoke** access for any connected application immediately without contacting support.
- **Audit** a timestamped log of every authorization, configuration change, and data exchange event for compliance purposes.

Each integration displays a status badge — **Active**, **Degraded**, or **Disconnected** — so your team can spot problems at a glance without digging through logs.

## Authorization and Security

Integrations that access or transmit patient data use encrypted, authenticated connections. OAuth 2.0 is used for consumer-facing applications; HL7 v2 and FHIR R4 interfaces are secured via VPN or TLS mutual authentication depending on the partner network. Every data exchange is logged and attributable to a specific integration credential, supporting your HIPAA audit trail requirements.

<Note>
  Any third-party application that receives, stores, or processes protected health information (PHI) on your behalf must have a signed **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** in place before you activate the integration. You can upload and manage BAA documents directly in **Settings → Integrations → [Partner Name] → Compliance Documents**. Activating an integration without a valid BAA may expose your organization to HIPAA liability.
</Note>
