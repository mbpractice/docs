Your EHR platform comes with dedicated support from a team of healthcare technology specialists who understand both the technical side of the software and the clinical workflows that depend on it. Whether you are dealing with a critical system issue or have a general question about a feature, this page explains how to reach the right channel quickly, what to expect in terms of response times, and how to get the most out of your support experience.

<Tip>
  The fastest way to get help for most issues is the **in-app help widget** — click the **?** icon in the bottom-right corner of any page in the EHR. The widget surfaces contextual help articles relevant to the page you are on, lets you search the full knowledge base, and connects you directly to live chat during support hours. Using the in-app widget also automatically attaches your account details, current page URL, and browser information to your request, which significantly speeds up triage.
</Tip>

## Severity Levels and Response SLAs

Every support request is assigned a severity level that determines the target response time and escalation path. Use the definitions below to identify the appropriate severity when submitting your ticket.

| Severity | Definition | Example Issues | Initial Response | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical (P1)** | Complete system outage or patient safety issue affecting all or most users | Platform is unreachable, all users locked out, data loss event, security breach | **1 hour** (24/7) | Active work until resolved |
| **High (P2)** | A key feature is unavailable or severely degraded, impacting clinical workflows with no workaround | E-prescribing down, lab results not routing, billing submissions failing | **4 hours** | Same business day |
| **Medium (P3)** | A feature is impaired but a workaround exists, or the issue affects a limited subset of users | Specific report not generating, calendar display error for one provider, intermittent slowness | **1 business day** | 3–5 business days |
| **Low (P4)** | General question, feature request, configuration help, or minor cosmetic issue | How do I customize a note template? Can I change my logo? | **3 business days** | Best effort; feature requests reviewed on a rolling basis |

<Note>
  Response times represent the time to first substantive response from a support agent — not necessarily full resolution. Resolution time depends on the complexity of the issue. For Critical and High issues, you will receive proactive status updates at least every hour until the issue is resolved. You can also follow real-time incident updates at **status.ehrplatform.com**.
</Note>

## How to Submit an Effective Support Ticket

A well-written ticket helps the support team skip the back-and-forth and get to a resolution faster. Include the following information in every ticket:

1. **Brief, descriptive subject line.** For example: "Lab results from Quest not routing to patient charts — started 2024-01-15" rather than "Lab problem."
2. **Your organization name and account ID.** Your account ID is visible in **Settings → Organization → Account Details**.
3. **Affected user(s).** Provide the usernames or roles of anyone experiencing the issue. Note whether the issue affects one user, a subset of users, or all users.
4. **What you expected to happen.** Describe what the correct behavior should be.
5. **What actually happened.** Describe the incorrect behavior in detail, including any error messages (copy the exact text or include a screenshot).
6. **Steps to reproduce.** List the exact steps that trigger the issue so the support team can replicate it on their end.
7. **When it started.** Note when you first observed the issue and whether anything changed around that time (new user added, browser update, etc.).
8. **What you have already tried.** Summarize any troubleshooting steps you took before submitting the ticket. This prevents the support team from suggesting steps you have already completed.
9. **Attachments.** Include screenshots, screen recordings, export files, or error logs. Use the annotation tools in the ticket portal to highlight relevant parts of screenshots.

## Platform Status Page

Before submitting a support ticket for platform-wide issues (such as slowness, login problems, or integration failures), check the status page to see if there is an active incident.

**Status page:** [status.ehrplatform.com](https://status.ehrplatform.com)

The status page displays real-time health information for all platform components — the web application, API, e-prescribing, lab integrations, billing/clearinghouse, and patient portal. When an incident is active, the team posts updates every 30–60 minutes with investigation findings, workarounds, and an estimated time to resolution.

Click **Subscribe to Updates** on the status page to receive incident notifications by email or SMS. You can subscribe to all components or only the ones most relevant to your workflows.
