Whether you are just getting started or have been using the platform for years, you are likely to have questions about how specific features work or how to handle common situations. This page collects the most frequently asked questions from healthcare providers across all specialties and practice sizes. If you do not find what you are looking for here, visit the [Common Issues](/support/common-issues) page for troubleshooting guidance, or [contact our support team](/support/contacting-support) directly.

  <Accordion title="How do I reset my password?">
    You can reset your password from the login screen without needing to contact support.

    1. Go to the EHR login page and click **Forgot Password?** below the sign-in form.
    2. Enter the email address associated with your account and click **Send Reset Link**.
    3. Check your inbox for an email from noreply@ehrplatform.com (check your spam folder if it does not arrive within a few minutes).
    4. Click the link in the email — it is valid for **60 minutes**.
    5. Enter and confirm your new password. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.

    If you have **multi-factor authentication (MFA)** enabled, you will still be prompted for your second factor after setting the new password. If you have also lost access to your MFA device, contact your organization administrator to reset your MFA, or reach out to [support](/support/contacting-support) if you are the sole administrator.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I import patient records from another EHR?">
    Yes. The platform supports importing patient records from other EHR systems in the following formats:

    - **FHIR R4 (JSON/XML)** — the preferred format for modern EHR-to-EHR migrations
    - **CCD / C-CDA (XML)** — compatible with most legacy EHR exports
    - **CSV** — for bulk importing demographics, appointment history, or simple structured data

    To start an import, go to **Settings → Data Management → Import Records** and follow the guided wizard. For large migrations (thousands of patients), the platform's **Migration Concierge** service is available to help you map fields, validate data, and perform a staged import with quality checks. Contact your account manager or support to engage the Migration Concierge team.

    Note that some data from other EHR systems — particularly proprietary note formats, custom forms, or binary attachments — may require manual review and cleanup after import. Allow adequate time for data validation before going live.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I set up a new provider?">
    Adding a new provider to your organization takes just a few minutes.

    1. Navigate to **Settings → Users & Roles → Users** and click **Add User**.
    2. Enter the provider's name, email address, NPI number, specialty, and preferred role (e.g., Physician, Nurse Practitioner, Physician Assistant).
    3. Assign the appropriate clinical permissions and any location or department restrictions.
    4. Click **Send Invitation**. The provider will receive an email inviting them to create their account and set up MFA.

    Once the provider completes their profile setup, they will appear in scheduling, clinical workflows, and e-prescribing. If the provider requires DEA registration for controlled substance prescribing, upload their DEA certificate under their provider profile in **Settings → Provider Credentials**. The platform integrates with Surescripts for EPCS (Electronic Prescribing of Controlled Substances) identity proofing — a one-time process required by DEA regulations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data backed up?">
    Yes. Your data is backed up automatically every **24 hours** using encrypted, geographically redundant backups stored across multiple availability zones within the United States. Additionally, continuous database transaction logs allow point-in-time recovery to within **5 minutes** of any data loss event.

    Backup retention periods:
    - **Daily backups** are retained for 35 days
    - **Weekly backups** are retained for 12 weeks
    - **Monthly backups** are retained for 12 months
    - **Annual backups** are retained for 7 years

    Backup restoration is performed by the platform operations team in the event of a disaster or data loss incident. You cannot self-service restore from backup, but you can export your data at any time via **Settings → Data Management → Bulk Export** as a working copy for your own records.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I access the EHR from my phone or tablet?">
    Yes. The platform is accessible from any modern mobile browser on iOS and Android devices. The mobile web interface is optimized for smaller screens and supports the most common clinical workflows, including:

    - Reviewing and responding to messages and task notifications
    - Viewing and updating patient schedules
    - Accessing patient charts, problem lists, and medication lists
    - Reviewing and signing clinical notes
    - Reviewing lab results and imaging reports

    For the best mobile experience, add the EHR to your device's home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA):
    - **iOS (Safari):** Tap the Share icon → **Add to Home Screen**
    - **Android (Chrome):** Tap the three-dot menu → **Add to Home Screen**

    This gives you an app-like experience with faster load times and offline access to recently viewed charts. Full clinical note authoring and complex workflows are best performed on a desktop or laptop.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does subscription billing work?">
    Subscriptions are billed on a **per-provider, per-month** basis. The number of billable providers is calculated on the first day of each billing cycle based on the number of active clinical users (Physician, NP, PA, and other prescribing roles) in your account. Administrative and front-desk users are not counted toward your provider seat total.

    - **Monthly billing:** Charged on the same day each month to your stored payment method.
    - **Annual billing:** Billed upfront for 12 months at a discounted rate. Annual plans are non-refundable after the first 30 days.
    - **Adding providers mid-cycle:** New providers added during a billing period are prorated for the remaining days in that cycle.
    - **Removing providers:** Removing a provider mid-cycle does not generate a credit; the reduction takes effect on the next billing cycle.

    View your invoices, update payment methods, and manage your subscription under **Settings → Billing & Subscription**. For questions about pricing, volume discounts, or contract terms, contact your account manager.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?">
    If you cancel your subscription, your account enters a **30-day grace period** during which you retain full read-only access to all your data. You can export patient records, reports, and any other data you need during this window.

    After the grace period:
    - Your account is deactivated and data access is suspended.
    - Your data remains in encrypted storage for an additional **60 days** (90 days total from cancellation) before permanent deletion begins.
    - You can reactivate your account at any time before the 90-day mark without data loss.

    If you need records retrieved after permanent deletion has begun, contact support immediately — recovery may be possible from backups depending on timing, but is not guaranteed.

    We strongly recommend exporting a full organizational dataset before cancelling. See [Data Export and Portability](/compliance/data-privacy#data-export-and-portability) for instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does onboarding take?">
    Onboarding timelines vary based on the size of your practice and whether you are migrating from an existing EHR. Typical timelines:

    | Practice Type | Typical Onboarding Timeline |
    |---|---|
    | Solo or small group (1–5 providers), new practice | 1–2 weeks |
    | Small to mid-size group (6–20 providers) | 3–6 weeks |
    | Mid-size group with data migration | 6–10 weeks |
    | Multi-location or large group | 10–16 weeks |

    Your onboarding includes: account configuration, user setup, payer and clearinghouse enrollment, lab integration, e-prescribing activation, and staff training. A dedicated onboarding specialist is assigned to your account and will guide you through each phase with a project plan and regular check-ins.

    The single biggest factor affecting onboarding duration is data migration complexity. Starting the data migration process as early as possible — ideally in parallel with configuration and training — shortens your overall timeline significantly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a patient portal?">
    Yes. The platform includes a fully integrated patient portal that gives your patients secure online access to their health information. Patients can use the portal to:

    - View their medical history, diagnoses, medications, and allergies
    - Review visit summaries and after-visit notes
    - View lab results as soon as you release them
    - Request prescription refills
    - Request and manage appointments
    - Complete intake forms and questionnaires before their visit
    - Send and receive secure messages with your care team
    - Review and pay bills online
    - Download their health records in standard formats

    To invite a patient to the portal, open their chart and click **Send Portal Invite** under the patient's contact information. The patient will receive an email or SMS invitation with setup instructions. You can configure which portal features are enabled and customize the portal with your practice's logo and contact information under **Settings → Patient Portal**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I connect to our reference laboratory?">
    The platform supports electronic lab ordering and result routing for all major national reference labs (Quest Diagnostics, LabCorp, BioReference, etc.) as well as hundreds of regional and hospital labs via the built-in lab interface engine.

    To connect a lab:
    1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Laboratory Interfaces**.
    2. Click **Add Lab Interface** and search for your lab by name.
    3. Enter your lab account number and any credentials provided by your lab representative.
    4. Select the interface type: **HL7 2.x**, **FHIR**, or **Direct Connection** (varies by lab).
    5. Click **Request Activation**. The platform's integrations team will contact the lab to complete the technical handshake — this typically takes **3–10 business days** depending on the lab's process.

    Once active, providers can place electronic orders directly from the patient chart under **Orders → Lab Orders**, and results route automatically into the patient's chart with provider notification. For STAT labs or labs not in the directory, contact support to initiate a custom interface setup.
  </Accordion>
