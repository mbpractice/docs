A thorough, up-to-date medical history is the foundation of safe, effective patient care. Your EHR consolidates every component of a patient's health background — from chronic conditions and current medications to family history and immunization records — into a single, structured Medical History section. Keeping this information current enables accurate clinical decision support, reduces risk, and streamlines every encounter note.

<Warning>
**Allergy documentation is a patient safety critical task.** Always document allergies using structured entries rather than free text so the system can trigger drug-allergy interaction alerts. Avoid noting allergies only in encounter notes or free-text fields — these entries are not checked by the clinical decision support engine. Confirm and reconcile allergy information at every visit.
</Warning>

## Accessing Medical History

Open a patient chart and click the **Medical History** tab. The section is divided into sub-categories accessible from the left-hand sidebar within the tab. Click any category to jump directly to it.

## Medical History Sections

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Problem List" icon="list-check">
    The problem list is a structured, prioritized list of the patient's active and historical diagnoses and chronic conditions.

    **To add a problem:**
    1. Click **+ Add Problem** in the Problem List section.
    2. Search for the condition using plain language (e.g., "type 2 diabetes") or ICD-10 code. The system returns matching coded results.
    3. Select the correct code from the dropdown.
    4. Set the **status**: Active, Inactive, Resolved, or Chronic.
    5. Enter the **onset date** (approximate dates are acceptable).
    6. Add a **clinical note** if needed for context.
    7. Click **Save**.

    **To edit a problem:** Click the pencil icon next to the condition, update the fields, and click **Save**.

    **To resolve or inactivate a problem:** Click the problem entry, change the status to **Resolved** or **Inactive**, enter the resolution date, and save. Resolved problems move to the **Historical Problems** section but remain visible.

    Problems marked **Active** or **Chronic** automatically populate the encounter note's Assessment section to streamline documentation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Medications" icon="pills">
    The medication list tracks all current, discontinued, and historical medications — including prescription, over-the-counter, and herbal/supplement entries.

    **To add a medication:**
    1. Click **+ Add Medication**.
    2. Search by drug name, NDC, or RxNorm code.
    3. Select the drug and choose the **dose**, **form**, **route**, and **frequency**.
    4. Set the **start date** and an **end date** if applicable.
    5. Select the **prescribing provider**.
    6. Add **patient instructions** or notes.
    7. Click **Save**.

    **To discontinue a medication:** Click the medication entry, set the status to **Discontinued**, enter the end date and reason, and save. Discontinued medications are retained in the medication history for reference.

    The system checks all new entries against the existing medication list and allergy records for interaction and contraindication alerts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Allergies" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Document all known drug, food, environmental, and contrast/dye allergies as structured entries.

    **To add an allergy:**
    1. Click **+ Add Allergy**.
    2. Select the **allergy type**: Drug, Food, Environmental, or Other.
    3. Search for the allergen by name or RxNorm/SNOMED code.
    4. Select the **reaction type** from the list (e.g., anaphylaxis, hives, nausea, rash).
    5. Set the **severity**: Mild, Moderate, or Severe.
    6. Enter the **onset date** if known.
    7. Add any additional clinical notes.
    8. Click **Save**.

    **To mark "No Known Allergies" (NKA):** Click the **NKA** button at the top of the Allergies section. This documents that allergies were reviewed and none are known — it is not the same as leaving the section blank.

    **To edit or remove an allergy:** Click the entry, update the fields or click **Delete**, and save. Deletion is logged in the audit trail.

    Active drug allergies display as a **red banner** in the patient chart header and trigger alerts during e-prescribing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Surgical History" icon="scalpel">
    Record past surgical procedures to inform clinical decision-making and pre-operative assessments.

    **To add a surgical history entry:**
    1. Click **+ Add Surgery**.
    2. Search for the procedure by name or CPT/SNOMED code.
    3. Enter the **date of surgery** (year is sufficient if the exact date is unknown).
    4. Record the **facility or surgeon** if known.
    5. Note any **complications** or relevant outcomes.
    6. Click **Save**.

    **To edit a surgical entry:** Click the pencil icon, update the relevant fields, and save. All edits are time-stamped.

    Surgical history entries are available in the encounter note template under the Past Surgical History (PSH) section and can be inserted into notes with one click.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Family History" icon="people-group">
    Document relevant hereditary conditions and family health patterns.

    **To add a family history entry:**
    1. Click **+ Add Family History**.
    2. Select the **relationship** (e.g., Mother, Father, Sibling, Grandparent).
    3. Search for the condition using plain language or ICD-10 code.
    4. Note whether the relative is **living or deceased**. If deceased, you can optionally record the **cause of death** and **age at death**.
    5. Click **Save**.

    You can add multiple conditions for the same family member. Entries are grouped by relationship in the display view.

    Family history findings that are relevant to hereditary risk (e.g., BRCA-related cancers, hereditary cardiac conditions) will trigger preventive care recommendations in the care gap engine.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Social History" icon="house-user">
    Capture lifestyle and social determinants of health that influence care planning.

    Social history is entered through a structured questionnaire. Click **Edit Social History** to open the form. Fields include:

    | Category | Details Captured |
    |---|---|
    | **Tobacco use** | Current, former, or never; type and quantity |
    | **Alcohol use** | Frequency, quantity, and AUDIT-C score |
    | **Substance use** | Recreational drug use, type, and frequency |
    | **Occupation** | Current occupation and industry |
    | **Exercise** | Frequency and type of physical activity |
    | **Diet** | Dietary restrictions or notable patterns |
    | **Living situation** | Living alone, with family, in assisted living, etc. |
    | **Sexual activity** | Number of partners, contraception use |
    | **Travel history** | Recent international travel relevant to infectious disease |

    Click **Save** to update the social history record. Changes are versioned so you can track how a patient's social circumstances evolve over time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Immunizations" icon="syringe">
    Track all administered and historical vaccines.

    **To add an immunization:**
    1. Click **+ Add Immunization**.
    2. Search for the vaccine using the CVX code or plain-language name.
    3. Enter the **date administered**.
    4. Select the **route** (IM, subcutaneous, oral, etc.) and **site** (left deltoid, right thigh, etc.).
    5. Enter the **lot number**, **manufacturer**, and **expiration date** for traceability.
    6. Select the **administering provider**.
    7. Click **Save**.

    **To document a historical vaccine** (administered outside your practice): Follow the same steps and toggle **Historically Reported** to indicate this was not administered in your clinic.

    The immunization module compares the patient's vaccine history against the current CDC recommended schedule and highlights any gaps or due immunizations in the **Care Gaps** panel.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reconciling History from Imported Records

When a patient's records are imported — via a Direct message, a Continuity of Care Document (CCD), or a manual document upload — the system places the imported data in a **Reconciliation Queue** rather than automatically updating the chart.

To reconcile imported history:

1. Open the patient chart and click the **Reconciliation** banner at the top of the Medical History tab. (This banner only appears when unreviewed imported data is present.)
2. The reconciliation view displays the **incoming data** on the left and the **current chart data** on the right, side by side.
3. For each section (Problems, Medications, Allergies, etc.), review each imported item and choose one of the following actions:
   - **Accept** — adds the item to the chart as a structured entry.
   - **Merge** — combines the imported item with an existing matching entry in the chart.
   - **Reject** — discards the imported item (the reason is logged in the audit trail).
4. Once all items in a section have been actioned, click **Complete Reconciliation** for that section.
5. Repeat for all sections, then click **Finalize Reconciliation** to mark the import as fully reviewed.

Reconciled data becomes part of the permanent patient record and is attributed to the clinician who performed the reconciliation.

<Note>
Reconciliation is required to satisfy Meaningful Use/Promoting Interoperability objectives when receiving patient health information from external sources. Your compliance dashboard tracks reconciliation completion rates.
</Note>
