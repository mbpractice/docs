The patient record is the central hub of clinical activity in your EHR. Every interaction — from a scheduled appointment to a lab result — is anchored to a single, longitudinal patient profile that gives your care team a complete, real-time view of each patient's health journey. Understanding how to navigate and manage patient records efficiently is essential for delivering high-quality, coordinated care.

## Patient Record Sections

Each patient record is organized into dedicated sections so your team can quickly locate the information they need. Use the tabs within the patient chart to move between sections.

## Searching and Filtering Patients

Use the **Patients** module in the top navigation bar to access your full patient panel. The patient list supports multiple search and filter methods to help you locate records quickly.

### Search by keyword

Type any of the following into the search bar to find a patient:

- Full name or partial name (first, last, or both)
- Date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY)
- Medical record number (MRN)
- Phone number or email address

Results appear as you type. Select a patient from the dropdown to open their full chart.

### Filter the patient list

Use the **Filters** panel on the left side of the Patients list to narrow results by:

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | Show patients assigned to a specific primary provider |
| **Last Visit** | Filter by date range of the most recent encounter |
| **Insurance Payer** | Display patients covered by a specific insurance plan |
| **Care Team Member** | Find patients where a given user appears on the care team |
| **Patient Status** | Active, inactive, deceased, or prospective patients |
| **Age Range** | Filter by minimum and maximum patient age |

Click **Apply Filters** to update the list. Use **Clear All** to reset to the default view.

### Sorting the patient list

Click any column header — such as **Last Name**, **Date of Birth**, or **Last Visit** — to sort the list ascending or descending by that field.

## Viewing a Patient Record

Click any patient's name in the search results or patient list to open their chart. The chart opens on the **Summary** tab, which provides an at-a-glance overview of active problems, current medications, upcoming appointments, and recent encounters.

Use the section tabs at the top of the chart to navigate to **Demographics**, **Insurance**, **Encounters**, **Medications**, **Labs**, **Documents**, and other areas.

### Chart summary banner

At the top of every patient chart, a persistent banner displays:

- Patient name, age, and date of birth
- MRN and primary insurance
- Known allergies (highlighted in red if critical)
- Preferred pharmacy
- Care team primary provider

This banner remains visible as you scroll through any section of the chart.

## Editing Patient Information

To update information in any section of the patient record:

1. Navigate to the relevant section tab.
2. Click the **Edit** button (pencil icon) in the top-right corner of the section.
3. Make your changes in the editable fields.
4. Click **Save** to commit the update.

All edits are timestamped and attributed to the logged-in user, creating a full audit trail.

<Tip>
Use the **Quick Actions** menu — the `⋮` icon in the top-right corner of the patient banner — to instantly access common tasks without leaving the chart. Quick actions include **New Appointment**, **New Encounter**, **Send Message**, **Print Summary**, **Request Lab**, and **Refill Medication**. This saves significant time during busy clinical sessions.
</Tip>

## Printing and Exporting Records

To generate a clinical summary or export records:

1. Open the patient chart.
2. Click **Quick Actions** → **Print Summary** or navigate to **Documents** → **Generate Report**.
3. Select the sections you want to include (e.g., Demographics, Problem List, Medications, Recent Labs).
4. Choose the output format: **Print**, **PDF Download**, or **Secure Fax**.
5. Click **Generate**.

The resulting document is automatically saved to the patient's **Documents** section for reference.

## Merging Duplicate Records

If a patient has been registered more than once, you can merge the duplicate records to consolidate their history. Navigate to **Admin** → **Duplicate Management** to review and action flagged duplicates. See [Registering Patients](/patients/registering-patients) for information on preventing duplicates at the point of registration.
